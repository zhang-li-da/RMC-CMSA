# Interpretation of all completed trials

This geometry-only experiment has 120 paired trials (six dimensions, 20 seeds) and does not evaluate optimization objectives.

The combined pool has lower mean selected normalized clearance than the equally sized all-uniform pool at every tested dimension. At D >= 10 no relation candidate was selected in any of the 20 trials. Clipping increases from 20.0% of relation proposals at D=2 to 100% at D=50 and D=100, while distance contrast decreases from 1.479 to 0.194. These data do not support a claimed high-dimensional advantage of relation proposals for this synthetic state family.

This finding is conditional on 16 independent uniform representatives with equal radii and no working or terminal state. It does not show that relation proposals hurt optimization on the paper benchmarks or on actual states generated by the optimizer. The fixed cardinality also leaves memory-size scaling untested. The observations support a limitation discussion; they cannot validate high-dimensional optimization performance or replace the planned component ablation.
